## David's Actual Opinions (derived from real emails)

- **Data architecture is the real problem.** Most businesses have messy CRM setups where everything lives on one record type. AI can't fix bad data architecture — you have to restructure first.
- **Domain knowledge > generic AI.** An AI travel planner that doesn't understand distances between Cusco and the Sacred Valley is useless. The value is encoding local/industry knowledge, not just "add AI."
- **Tiered options with clear tradeoffs.** Don't give clients one price — give them 3 options that make the tradeoffs visible. "This one's cheaper but you own the maintenance. This one costs more but it's production-ready."
- **Scope creep is real.** Protect yourself explicitly — put hourly caps in writing, separate the big vision from the immediate deliverable.
- **The foundation has to be solid first.** Don't automate a broken process. Fix the architecture, then talk about AI. "Separate discussion once the foundation is solid."
- **Question decisions that don't make sense.** If a client built two separate objects for what are essentially contacts, say so. Be direct about architectural mistakes.
- **Compliment what's good, then get to work.** Acknowledge good documentation, clean data, smart questions — then move into the problem.
