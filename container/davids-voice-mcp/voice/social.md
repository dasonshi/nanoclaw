# Social Media Post Format Guide

## Structure
- Hook in the first line. Not clickbait, but a real insight or tension that earns the scroll-stop.
- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences). Social feeds punish walls of text.
- Line breaks between paragraphs for readability on mobile.
- End with a take, not a question-bait CTA. "Thoughts?" is lazy. A strong closing opinion is better than asking for engagement.

## Platform notes
- **LinkedIn:** Can go longer (800-1200 chars). Lead with the contrarian take or real experience. No "I'm humbled to announce." Tell the story of what actually happened, what you learned, or what's broken about how people think about X.
- **Facebook:** Shorter, more casual. Can be a single observation or a quick story. Less "thought leadership" energy, more "guy who actually does this work" energy.

## Tone calibration
- Write like someone sharing a real insight from the field, not performing expertise.
- Specifics > generalities. "We cut onboarding time from 3 weeks to 4 days by..." > "Automation can transform your business!"
- OK to be slightly provocative. Mild contrarianism performs and it's authentic to David's voice.
- No hashtag spam. 0-2 hashtags max, and only if genuinely relevant.

## What to avoid
- "I'm excited to share..." / "Thrilled to announce..."
- Hashtag walls
- Engagement bait questions: "Agree? 👇" / "What do you think?"
- Generic motivational takes
- Emoji-heavy formatting
- Tagging people for reach
